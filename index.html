Sources & disclosure

All data on this map was compiled from publicly available sources. This is a personal hobby visualization — not an official county product, not a title search, and not legal advice. Always verify with Loudoun County records before relying on any sale, owner, or boundary detail.

Parcel boundaries

  • Loudoun County GIS — Land Records / Parcel Boundaries (PA_SUBD_NAME='BROADLANDS' and PA_SUBD_SECT='13')
  • Service: logis.loudoun.gov … LandRecords/MapServer/5

Sales & ownership history

  • Loudoun County real estate / tax assessment sales history (public parcel records)
  • County assessment & land records portals (Loudoun Online Tax System / LandMARC where used for deed context)

Basemap

  • Streets: © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Satellite: Esri World Imagery Wayback archive — imagery release tracks the scrubber year (archive from ~2014; earlier years use the oldest available vintage)

Notes

  • Ownership eras are inferred from recorded sale/deed rows we pulled; $0 / trust transfers still count as owner-era changes.
  • Scrubber is year-level (end of year), not day-level.
  • A few GIS parcels may lack matching sales rows in the local extract.
